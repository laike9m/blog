Many people want to use Clicknow and [PopClip](https://www.popclip.app/) together, in this article I'm gonna show you how. 

## **Recommended: use Clicknow's [PopClip extension](https://github.com/laike9m/Clicknow/releases/download/1.14.8/clicknow.popcliptxt)**

Once installed, Clicknow can be triggered from PopClip:

![](/blog/assets/uploads/621b2d759e5596a40b98dd9a645aeea1.png)

You still need to have Clicknow installed and activated, but you can disable it to avoid having two popups. [Read more here](/blog/popclip-clicknow-extension).

## Alternative: having two popups

I crafted Clicknow so that its popup doesn't overlap with PopClip's, and can be used together.
