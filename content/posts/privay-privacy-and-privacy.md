**In Clicknow, user privacy is paramount.**

### What Information Does Clicknow Collects?

**When you click the search or translate button,** **only the selected text is sent to Clicknow's server**. We don't collect device info, IP, or any other personal Identifiable Information (PII).

Note, that the text is only sent upon clicking on the button, or pressing a shortcut. It is NOT sent when you highlight the text, so rest assured.

### What Information Is Stored on Clicknow's Server?

The only information stored is user **payment info** and **activation status**.   
Clicknow does **NOT** store user-selected text.

### Can Clicknow Link Requests to A Specific User?

**No**. We authenticate requests in an anonymous way, making it impossible to link to a specific user even if we want to.
