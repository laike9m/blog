In 1.13.4, Clicknow gains a revamped spell checker. Here's what we improved:

  • Previously, the spell checker only works in limited places. Now, it works everywhere: email, doc, notes, chat, webpage, you name it
  • It now provides multiple choices (previously just one)
  • If you think the spelling is correct, you can add it to dictionary, so it won't be recognized as typo any more.

Hope you find the new spell checker useful 😃
