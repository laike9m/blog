Clicknow vs Dictionary

Compared to dictionary, Clicknow let you access the world's knowledge, no matter if it's an academic term or the latest slang.

Clicknow vs Perplexity

Clicknow and Perplexity both provides online AI-search features. What sets Clicknow apart from Perplexity is its seamless integration and ease of use. Unlike Perplexity which is a standalone website, Clicknow works within any app you're using. With just one click, you can get an instant explanation of selected text in your preferred language - no need to copy text, open a new browser tab, and paste the text. This makes it incredibly convenient for quick translations, research while reading PDFs, browsing the web, or using any other app.

Clicknow vs Apple Intelligence

While Apple Intelligence focuses on AI-assisted writing, Clicknow is designed for instant searches and explanations. With Clicknow, you can quickly get detailed information about any term or phrase you come across, without disrupting your workflow. Its AI-powered search provides accurate, real-time explanations with additional context, giving you a deeper understanding of the selected text. In contrast, Apple Intelligence primarily offers writing suggestions and assistance, making it less suitable for on-the-spot research and translations.

Clicknow vs PopClip

Everyone loves PopClip. When it comes to extensibility, PopClip is the best.

Clicknow tries to do one thing and one thing really well: AI-search. While you can use PopClip to Google the selected text, it's still one step away in a new browser tab. With Clicknow, you can get the information you need while staying in your current app, without being interrupted, and no need to switching between apps.
